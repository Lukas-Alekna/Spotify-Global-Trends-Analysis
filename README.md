# Spotify-Global-Trends-Analysis
Data processing and analysis of a 100k+ row Spotify dataset to aggregate music streaming volume, track seasonal trends, and isolate audio popularity drivers.

Conclusions 

Track popularity  
The Top 3 most popular tracks are: Pop-film, K-pop and Chill.  
● Pop-film average popularity is 59.28 
● K-pop average popularity is 56.90 
● Chill average popularity is 53.65 

Insights: 
Pop-film tracks are the most popular, because they are introduced to a wide audience around the world.  
These tracks are mostly used in movie industry, then we can make a conclusion that movies popularity 
directly correlates with music popularity. For example: more popular movie- more popular tracks, which 
were played. 

Another insight would be K-pop music popularity. This shows a clear trend in todays music industry. 
K-pop music is popular among young people and ofcourse Koreans. K-pop is not niche music anymore by 
any means. Its even above Pop music which average popularity is 47.58 (10th place), while K- pop is at 
second place with average popularity being 56.90.  

On another hand, chill music still hits top of the charts, because it is used by a wide audience, no matter 
the age. Chill music could be listened to: at work, school, doing homework, at home and ect. So people in 
almost every situation can listen to Chill music, as it is not as distracting and could be calming or 
comforting.  

Track duration  
The longest track on the chart is Minimal-techno (Max duration: 5237295 ms (or 87.29 minutes)). 
The shortest track on the chart is Iranian genre music (Min duration: 8586ms (or 8.586 seconds)). 
Meanwhile, the longest average duration is Detroit-techno (Duration 372012ms (or 6.20 minutes)). 
The shortest average duration is Grindcore (Duration 133754ms (or 2.23 minutes)). 
By these numbers we can see that the longest tracks are techno based music. This could be, because 
techno music has many repeated patterns and not that much vocals. 

Energy and Danceability 

As we can see in the chart (on page “Energy And Danceability Chart”) there are various genres of music, 
with really different indexes of danceability and energy. 

Genres of music that Danceability and Energy indexes are almost the same: Afrobeat, Blues, British, 
Country, Dance, Deep-House, Detroit- Techno, Electro, German, Idm, Indian, J-dance, Latin, K-pop, 
Minimal-techno, Opera, Pop, Pop-film, Reggae, Raggaeton, Rock-n-roll, Trip-hop, Turkish. 
While the biggest differences has: Black-metal (Danceability- 0.296, Energy- 0.875), Death Metal 
(Danceability-0.368, Energy-0.931), Grindcore (Danceability- 0.272, Energy- 0924), Metal core 
(Danceability- 0.424, Energy- 0.914). This suggests that the most energy having, but least danceable 
music is Metal. While more traditional music styles like: Blues, Country, Dance have danceability and 
energy at almost the same index, Metal is completely different.
